Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

Who we are

Zivo is operated by PRASIDH KUMAR SETH. For any question about this policy or your data, write to prasidhseth706@gmail.com .

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, role (rider or driver), an optional phone number, and a hashed version of your password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Rides: pickup and drop-off names and coordinates, distance, fare, status, timestamps, whether the ride was paid, a trip code used to start the ride, and any rating you give.
  • Ride route: the driver's position points, recorded while a ride is active. Administrators can view them.
  • Driver location: while a driver is online, the last position the app reported.
  • Driver vehicle: the vehicle name, colour and number plate a driver enters. Riders see these during a ride.
  • Support: messages you send to support and our replies.
  • Admin records: a log of administrator actions, such as suspending an account or resolving an SOS alert.
  • Safety: a mark on a ride if a rider or driver sends an SOS alert.

How we use it

We use this data to run the service: to create your account, calculate fares, match riders with drivers, show ride progress, record payments and ratings, and handle safety alerts.

During a ride, the rider can see the driver's name, rating, vehicle details and live location, and the driver can see the pickup and drop-off. Administrators can view accounts (including phone numbers), rides, support messages and the route recorded for each ride to operate the service and respond to SOS alerts.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not run analytics.
  • We do not set cookies. Your login token is kept in your browser's session storage and is removed when you log out or close the tab.

Third-party services

To work, the app contacts these services from your browser. They can see your IP address and the data listed.

  • OpenStreetMap: map tiles for the area you view.
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap): the place name you search, or the coordinates you tap, to find an address.
  • OSRM: the pickup and drop-off coordinates, to draw the route.
  • Google Fonts: font files for the app.
  • unpkg and jsDelivr: the open source map and messaging libraries the app loads.

Each service has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep data

We keep account and ride records while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data, except records we must keep by law. Send the request to prasidhseth706@gmail.com .

Security

Passwords are hashed with BCrypt. Access to a ride is limited to its rider, its driver and administrators. Traffic is sent over HTTPS on the live site. No system is completely secure, so use a strong, unique password.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data by writing to prasidhseth706@gmail.com . Zivo is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
