Rides and fares

The fare is calculated by our server from the estimated road distance and is shown before you book. The booked fare is the fare you pay. The driver starts the trip by entering the trip code the rider is given. You can cancel a ride before the trip starts.

Payment

The rider pays after the trip is completed. The app records the payment against the ride. The driver receives a share of the fare and Zivo keeps the rest. Drivers see their share for each trip in the app.
